01 / Our approach

More than a roster of specialists.
A team that sees the whole game.

Great games require more than individual skills. They require a shared understanding of gameplay, technology, art, and player experience. At Candy Smith, we approach development as a complete production process. Our compact team structure lets us work closely with clients, make decisions efficiently, and bring together the right expertise for every project.
